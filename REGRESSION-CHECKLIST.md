# Hallways — physical regression checklist (approved magic)

Run on device after any change to critters, navigation, elevator or onboarding.
Start each run with **Dev → RESET ONBOARDING / NEW PLAYER** unless noted.
Watch the Xcode console for `[Cast]`, `[Lead]` and `[ELEVATOR] [Guide]` lines.

## Onboarding — Administration (Floor 2)
1. **Kiosk first (pre-ID).** Before the kiosk, open Jeff's office door. He is standing there: ears up, "tink?", head-shake + "nuh-uh" + stamp, tail flag up, slips out past you, door shuts behind him, leads to the kiosk, taps it, looks back/calls until you arrive, "mrp", goes home, door shuts.
   - Ignore him ~8 s → one escalation, then patience. Tap the door while he's out → "nuh-uh" from him.
2. **ID card.** Kiosk photo → accept → take card (admission completes). Door guard is gone; Jeff is at his desk.
3. **Desk encounter.** Notice, double-take, recognition, lamp off, hop down, round the desk, report.
4. **Follow me.** Look up, flag up, come-along call, two hops, wind-up; trots past you, noses the office door open (TONK), waits just outside looking back.
5. **Floor 2 lead.** Waits at the counter junction toward the glass doors; opens them; vestibule; settles beside the elevator facing it, glancing back now and then. Escalation once if you stall.

## Guided journey
6. **Boarding.** Tap the elevator: Jeff slips into the cab ahead of you, stands by the doors (side away from the panel), tail up.
7. **Auto press.** After the panel pivot, Floor 3 presses itself (cap pushes, click, light).
8. **Jeff in the ride.** Free-look during the ride finds him in the cab.
9. **Elevator sequence (untouched).** Free look, timing, preload/handoff, ding, doors, arrival landing straight out the doorway, doorway exit through the opening, touch behaviour.
10. **Floor 3 lead (office floor).** Out first; looks back + calls just outside; waits at the mouth of the north corridor; pauses mid-corridor only if you fall well behind; at the top junction, mouth of the east branch; round the corner; down the east corridor to YOUR door (steel nameplate = your employee number). He noses it open and waits just inside.
11. **The office.** Desk facing the door with the banker's lamp OFF, computer, your ID portrait framed on the desk, chair, visitor chair, filing cabinet, near-empty shelf, coat rack with one bare hanger, one photo on the wall, one bare nail by the door. Quiet floor: no critters, no Freddy.
12. **Goodbye.** He hops onto the desk, switches YOUR lamp ON, flag down, taps the desk twice, hops down, comes to you, bows + wags (a tap = happy shimmy; he does NOT join you), steps back, leaves through the door, one last look back.
13. **Departure.** He walks back to the elevator on his own; the elevator dings, opens for him, closes -- he's gone (from the office you only hear the ding). You can't board during his door cycle. No companion follows you anywhere afterwards; he is at his Orientation desk on Floor 2.

## Office computer (Floor 3, your office)
13a. **Sit down (physical monitor).** In the office, tap the computer (or desk, or desk chair): the view glides round the desk into the chair and stops looking at the WHOLE monitor with room on both sides (portrait), desk and room visible around it. The desktop is already on the monitor -- no zoom, no full-phone takeover. Rotate the phone while seated: the chair re-adjusts and the desktop stays on the monitor.
13b. **Get up / freedom.** No Leave Desk button. Tap the room outside the monitor (wall, desk, lamp...): you rise, go round the open end of the desk and stop out in the room facing the door; the next tap walks normally. A tap on the monitor's bezel/keyboard does nothing. Nothing opens by itself (no Mail popping up after Candy leaves). Works from the desktop, inside Mail and mid-game. The elevator works as normal afterwards.
13c. **Mail (on the monitor).** First sit-down: Inbox (side by side with the open message) has 4 messages (Administration, Facilities, IT Services, Recreation Committee), unread dots, red badge on the Mail icon. Open one: subject, sender + address, To: your number, date/time, body. Badge count drops. Relaunch → read state kept.
13d. **Games (on the monitor).** Games → 6 colour games (Woidle, Solitaire, Video Poker, Letterhead, Minefield, Merger, Paper Jam, Inbox Zero, Fragile, Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Hangman). Fragile: a 9x4 sheet of bubble wrap on cardboard; touch a bubble = it pops on touch-down (squash, snap, ring + glints, flat creased crater, panned pop sound, light haptic); drag across the sheet pops every bubble you pass; quick pops climb in pitch; ~1 stubborn bubble per sheet squishes + squeaks on the first press and bangs on the second; last bubble -> sheet peels away left and a new one slides in (sometimes pink anti-static or blue); footer tally "N popped" + sheet number; no score, timer, fail or New button; the half-popped sheet is kept when you leave and come back. Inbox Zero: envelopes march side to side and drop a row at each edge (typewriter clacks speed up as fewer remain); left pads < > (hold to slide), right Fire pad (tap = one reply, hold = keep replying; max 2 in flight); hits pop + rising pluck, streak builds x2/x3/x4 (a miss resets it); falling ! marks can be dodged or shot; red URGENT envelope crosses the top sometimes; hit = red flash + shake, tray blinks; 3 trays; envelopes reaching the dotted line = "Inbox full.", no trays = "Out of office."; clearing a wave = "Inbox zero!" + bonus, then "Wave N" pause; everything pauses when the game is closed, the ? card is up or the app is in the background; New run needs a second tap mid-run (no title-bar New game). Paper Jam: 8x14 well; left pads < v > (hold to repeat), right pads turn + Drop; ghost shows the landing; rows flash then clear; frame turns red when the stack is high; falling pauses when the game is closed or the ? card is up; New run needs a second tap mid-run. Merger: 4x4, swipe on the board or tap the arrow pad; frame warms amber/red as cells run out; merges pop + pitched sound; 128+ flashes its number; New board needs a second tap mid-run. Minefield: 5x9, 8 mines, first tap always opens ground; tap = reveal, long press = flag (always), Flag switch makes taps flag (orange frame); clock runs only while the game is open; New field (no title-bar New game here). Letterhead: 6-letter rack, 75 s real-deadline clock (keeps running while closed; reopening after it ran out shows the end screen), tap tiles / delete / Clear / Shuffle / Enter, 1-2-4-8 scoring, end screen shows score, the six-letter word, found + missed words, New rack. Video Poker (Jacks or Better): pay-table ladder above the cards; tap cards to HOLD (tint border + HOLD tag, blip), Draw (zip) once, result announced big in the strip (pair of 10s or lower = NO WIN) with an escalating sound (dud … royal ~4 s); New hand (no title-bar New game here); closing and reopening keeps the exact hand. Solitaire: tap the deck to deal one (empty deck = turn the waste over); tap a card to pick it up (gold outline), tap a column/foundation to put it down; tap the picked-up card again to send it home; win → "All 52 home." + Play again. Woidle (green/gold/grey feedback): a non-word shakes the row + "Not in the word list"; a win/loss stays up until Play again; the Woidle floor terminal still unlocks its floor exactly as before. With a game open there is ONE bar: ‹ All games · title · New game · ×. A finished game (win, loss or draw) stays on screen until you choose Play again / New game. Floor terminal versions (incl. Rock Paper Scissors, Higher or Lower) still behave exactly as before on their own floors -- Tic-Tac-Toe there still resets itself after a loss/draw.
13e. **History.** Console/recorder shows `history.record` lines: computer.session.began/ended (with duration), mail.opened/closed, mail.message.read (mail ID), games.opened/closed, game.launched / game.exited (game ID + duration).

## Freddy (any non-onboarding floor, post-prologue)
14. **Recruit.** Tap Freddy → he follows (beside you, rounds corners on your trail).
15. **Fire.** On the fire floor he goes to a fire, aims, sprays it out, comes back.
16. **Elevator.** Ride with him following → he's beside you in the arriving cab.

## Always
16a. **Elevator call button (every floor).** Standing at the elevator outside the doors, tap the brass call panel (either button): same result as tapping the doors -- no wall thump.
17. **Footsteps.** Synthesized player footsteps (shoe/heel loops) during every walk.
18. **Relaunch.** Mid-onboarding relaunch resumes in Administration with the right Jeff (door before ID, desk after).
19. **One Jeff.** At every floor build the console shows exactly one `[Cast] floor N ready -- jeff: …` line and no `WARNING` lines.
